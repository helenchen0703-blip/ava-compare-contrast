# Ava Compare & Contrast Worktable

Interactive worktable for Ava (Grade 4, multilingual learner) to practice compare & contrast writing using the **BRIDGE Learning Support** framework.

## Task
> Compare and contrast life in Ancient Athens and Ancient Sparta. Include details from both texts.

## Worktable Steps
1. **Vocabulary** — flip cards to check key words (city-state, trade, soldier, compare, contrast...)
2. **Sort It** — T-chart: green = same, orange = different
3. **House Map** — T–I–I–I–D–D–D–E graphic organizer
4. **Build Sentences** — sentence starters with fill-in blanks
5. **Write!** — final paragraph + self-checklist
6. **Glow & Grow** — reflect on strengths and growth
